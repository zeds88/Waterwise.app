TERMS

1. Term. Monthly plans run month to month from the start date. Either party may end the agreement with 30 days’ written notice (email or text). Annual plans run 12 months; on early cancellation Waterline refunds unused months less a true-up of benefits already used at their standalone value.

2. Invoicing. The monthly fee is invoiced on the 1st and due net 15 by Zelle (danny@waterlinedfw.com), ACH, or check. Repairs are invoiced on completion, net 15. Balances more than 30 days past due pause included visits until current.

3. Site walk scope. Run all zones; inspect heads, coverage, and overspray; inspect valves, wiring, controller, and sensor; set the program to the city schedule and season; note standing water and drain outlets. Minor adjustments (heads, nozzles, arcs, program) are included; a photo report with flat-rate repair pricing follows each visit.

4. Repairs and approvals. Repairs are quoted at published flat rates less 10%. Core and Managed: written approval (text or email) before work. Full Service: repairs up to $250 per visit are pre-approved and itemized on the report. Waterline may shut off a mainline break without approval to protect the property.

5. Response times. Core within 48 hours, Managed within 24 hours, Full Service the same business day for mainline breaks and standing water. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 7 am to 6 pm, weather and access permitting.

6. Monitoring (Managed, Full Service). Requires a Wi-Fi controller (Hunter Hydrawise or central control) with Waterline added as contractor. Waterline reviews alerts, adjusts programs remotely, and notifies the contact of faults. Waterline is not responsible for Wi-Fi, power, or vendor platform outages.

7. Drain cleaning (Managed, Full Service). Quarterly: clear surface drains, catch basins, downspout tie-ins, and outlets accessible from grade; report condition with photos. Excavation, pipe repair, regrading, and root removal are quoted separately.

8. Water restrictions. Controllers are programmed to the property’s city watering schedule and the season, and every program change is noted on the report. The client is responsible for fines arising from program changes made by the client or others.

9. Access. The client provides gate codes, controller access, and the water shutoff location. A visit missed for lack of access is rescheduled once within the same month.

10. Insurance and compliance. Waterline Drain & Sprinkler, LLC operates under Texas Licensed Irrigator LI0025754 and carries general liability insurance; a certificate of insurance is available on request. City-required backflow testing is coordinated with a licensed backflow prevention assembly tester and billed at the tester’s rate.

11. Warranty. Waterline warrants its workmanship on repairs for 1 year (Core, Managed) or 2 years (Full Service). Parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty. Excluded: freeze, vandalism, vehicle damage, work by others, utility work, or changes to water pressure or supply.

12. Not included. Backflow or mainline replacement, controller replacement, new zones, drainage installation, landscape and bed work, and any single repair over $1,500 are quoted separately; the plan discount applies.

13. Service area and portfolios. Pricing applies within 20 miles of ZIP 75039; beyond that a $25 travel add-on per visit applies. Portfolio pricing for three or more properties is quoted on request.

14. Regulation. Irrigation in Texas is regulated by the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ), MC-178, P.O. Box 13087, Austin, Texas 78711-3087, www.tceq.texas.gov. Work is performed by or under the supervision of Danny Mastroni, Licensed Irrigator LI0025754.

How pricing works Every number on this page is a published flat rate. The monthly fee is the full cost of the plan: no setup fee, no per-visit charge, no cancellation fee. Repairs are priced at flat rate less 10% on the report you receive after each walk, and nothing outside your approval threshold happens until you say go. No surprises.
