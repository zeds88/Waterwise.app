TERMS

1. Term. Monthly plans run month to month from the start date and continue until cancelled. Annual plans run 12 months and renew at the then-current published rate unless either party gives notice 30 days before renewal; Waterline texts a reminder about 45 days out. A membership year is 12 months from the start date.

2. Payment. Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month by Zelle to danny@waterlinedfw.com (set up as a recurring payment in your banking app) and are due within 5 days. Annual plans are due at signing. Membership is active when the first payment clears. No enrollment fees, no per-visit charges for included visits.

3. Scheduling. Waterline contacts the member to schedule each visit inside the seasonal windows above. Members receive scheduling priority over non-member requests. A visit missed for lack of access may be rescheduled once within the same window.

4. Tune-up scope. Run all zones; inspect heads, nozzles, arcs, and coverage; inspect valves, controller programming, and rain/freeze sensor; reset seasonal run times; walk visible drainage. Minor adjustments (head straightening, nozzle cleaning, arc and radius adjustment, program changes) are included. Replacement parts and repairs beyond adjustment are quoted at published flat rates less 10% and performed only with the member’s approval.

5. Service calls. Member-requested visits carry no service-call fee; diagnostic time is included. Repairs are quoted and approved before work. Premium members receive a response and a scheduled time by the next business day, weather and site access permitting.

6. Hydrawise monitoring (Recommended, Premium). Requires a Hunter Hydrawise controller on the member’s Wi-Fi with Waterline added as contractor. Waterline reviews alerts, adjusts programs remotely, and notifies the member of faults. Waterline is not responsible for Wi-Fi, power, or Hunter service outages.

7. Drain flush (Recommended, Premium). Once per term: flush and inspect surface drains, catch basins, downspout tie-ins, and French drain outlets accessible from grade; clear debris; report condition with photos. Excavation, pipe repair, regrading, and root removal are quoted separately.

8. Repair credit (Premium). $150 per term toward repair parts and labor, applied after the member discount. No cash value; unused credit does not roll over.

9. Warranty. Waterline warrants its workmanship on member repairs for 1 year (Essential, Recommended) or 2 years (Premium). Parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty. Excluded: damage from freeze, digging, vehicles, pets, work by others, utility work, or changes to water pressure or supply.

10. Not included. Backflow or mainline replacement, controller replacement, new zones, drainage installation, sod, and landscape work are quoted separately; the member discount applies. City-required backflow testing is scheduled separately where applicable.

11. Service area. Pricing applies within 20 miles of ZIP 75039. Beyond that, a $20 travel add-on per visit applies and is noted at signing.

12. Cancel anytime. Cancel any time in writing (a text is fine). Monthly billing stops at the end of the current month; annual plans are refunded for unused months. If a membership ends before month 12, benefits already used are trued up at their standalone value (visits $85 each, drain flush $150, repair credit as used) less the amount paid, never more than the balance of the year. Waterline may cancel for non-payment or unsafe site conditions on the same basis.

13. Transfer (Premium). If the home is sold, the remaining term transfers to the new owner at no charge once Waterline receives the new owner’s contact details.

14. Regulation. Irrigation in Texas is regulated by the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ), MC-178, P.O. Box 13087, Austin, Texas 78711-3087, www.tceq.texas.gov. Work is performed by or under the supervision of Danny Mastroni, Licensed Irrigator LI0025754.

How pricing works Every number on this page is a published flat rate. The monthly fee is the full cost of the plan: no enrollment fee, no per-visit charge, no cancellation fee, no automatic upsells. If a visit turns up something that needs a repair, you get the flat rate less 10% in writing, and nothing happens until you say go. No surprises.
